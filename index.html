01 — The Workshop

One banner, every build

03NET-WRT is a small home-internet & voucher-hotspot business — and everything it ships carries the same signature: Powered by 03NET.

Built for production, not demos

Every firmware, app and kernel here runs on real hardware serving real customers — PPPoE home internet and voucher hotspots managed through OpenWrt routers in Kabupaten Bandung. If it breaks at 2 AM, it gets fixed.

No bloat, no shortcuts

Branded properly from source — never rebadged binaries. Open-source forks, honest GPL compliance, and builds reproducible through GitHub Actions. What you see is what runs.
